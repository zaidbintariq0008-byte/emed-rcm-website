Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how eMed RCM Solutions ("eMed RCM Solutions", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information obtained through this website and our revenue cycle management services.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide through our inquiry and audit request forms, including your name, professional email, phone number, practice name, specialty, and the services you are interested in. We also collect standard technical information such as browser type and pages visited.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide requested revenue cycle reviews.
  • To deliver and improve our medical billing and RCM services.
  • To communicate about your account, service updates, and relevant offerings.

Protected Health Information

Any Protected Health Information (PHI) handled in the course of our services is processed strictly in accordance with HIPAA and the Business Associate Agreement in place with each client. PHI is never collected through this public website's forms, and website visitors should not submit patient information through them.

Data Security

We protect information with 256-bit SSL encryption in transit, strict role-based access controls, and SOC 2 aligned operational processes.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers who support our operations under confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

Your Choices

Changes to This Policy
