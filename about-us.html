About eMed RCM Solutions

A revenue cycle partner built by billing professionals, trusted by practices across the United States since 2012.

Our Story

Built on Billing Expertise, Grown on Client Trust

eMed RCM Solutions was founded in 2012 with a simple observation: too many good practices were working harder than ever while collecting less than they earned. Claims were denied for fixable reasons, AR was aging quietly, and providers were spending evenings on paperwork instead of with their families.

We built a company to change that. Today, headquartered in Naperville, IL, our team of experienced billers, certified coders and AR specialists serves more than 200 clients and 500 providers across 40+ states, recovering over $50 million in revenue that practices had already earned. We keep learning and implementing the latest ideas and processes in medical billing, so our clients can concentrate on their core work, patient care, and outsource the rest to safe hands.
