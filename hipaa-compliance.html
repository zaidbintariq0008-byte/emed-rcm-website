Compliance

HIPAA Compliance

eMed RCM Solutions is committed to full compliance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and the HITECH Act. As a Business Associate to every client practice, we handle Protected Health Information (PHI) under signed Business Associate Agreements and the safeguards described on this page.

Administrative Safeguards

  • Signed Business Associate Agreement with every client before any PHI is exchanged.
  • Designated privacy and security officers and documented risk assessments.
  • Regular HIPAA training and certification for every team member who touches PHI.

Technical Safeguards

  • 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit and encrypted storage at rest.
  • Role-based access controls: team members see only the minimum necessary information.
  • Audit logging and monitoring across our HIPAA compliant encrypted cloud pipeline.
  • SOC 2 Type II aligned operational processes.

Physical Safeguards

  • Restricted access workstations and secure facilities for all billing operations.
  • No PHI is stored on personal devices; all work happens inside controlled systems.

Breach Notification

In the event of any suspected breach involving PHI, eMed RCM Solutions notifies the affected client without unreasonable delay, in accordance with the HIPAA Breach Notification Rule and the terms of our Business Associate Agreement.

Important Notice

Please do not send patient information, medical records, or other PHI through this website's public inquiry forms or by unencrypted email. Clients exchange PHI only through the secure channels established during onboarding.
